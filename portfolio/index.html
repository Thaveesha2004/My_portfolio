About Me

about_thaveesha.md

I'm an undergraduate reading for a BSc (Hons) in Management and Information Technology at the University of Kelaniya, with my sights set on becoming a Cybersecurity Analyst. My interest in the field started with wanting to understand how systems actually break — and it's grown into a focused interest in network security, cloud security, and real-world vulnerability analysis.

Alongside coursework, I've worked on hands-on projects spanning full-stack development, database design, and systems programming — most recently handling backend development, authentication, and DevOps for Taskora, a real-time task management platform built with a five-person team. I'm also building my foundations in security tooling, including Kali Linux, Wireshark, and Cisco Packet Tracer.

I work well both independently and in teams, and I care about writing clean, well-structured code and communicating clearly — a skill I've deliberately sharpened through my university's Personal Progress Development (PPD) programme alongside the technical side of my degree.
